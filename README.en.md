## 👤 About me

I work across the whole stack —**frontend**, **backend** and **process automation**—, although what I enjoy most is the **frontend**. I like turning ideas into functional products and taking care of every detail of the result.

- 📚 **Continuous learning:** always adding new tools and best practices.
